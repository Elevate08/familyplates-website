Thoughtful by default

Built around the way families actually eat.

No feeds, streaks, or meal-planning theater. Just practical tools that make the week easier.

MEAL PLANNER

See the whole week at a glance.

Plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Assign a cook, choose a time, and turn leftovers into tomorrow’s plan.

RECIPE VAULT

Keep every family favorite close.

Import recipes from the web, browse by meal or tag, and let everyone share what they’re craving.

FRIDGE READY

A plan everyone can see.

Print a clean, high-contrast weekly or monthly calendar on one sheet.

SMART GROCERY LIST

From plan to aisle.

Ingredients are combined, grouped by aisle, and checked against pantry staples.

PANTRY SHIELD

Shop for what you need—not what you have.

Keep an aisle-organized inventory of everyday staples. Items marked on hand stay off the weekly grocery list automatically.

GOOGLE CALENDAR SYNC

Put every meal on the family calendar.

Sync scheduled meals automatically, including meal times, cook assignments, preparation time, and recipe details.

Your kitchen. Your data.

Made to live at home.

FamilyPlates is open-source and self-hosted. Run it on your own hardware, keep your family data under your control, and shape it around the way your household works.

  • ✓ Single-container Docker setup
  • ✓ SQLite with persistent storage
  • ✓ Built with Rails, Hotwire, and Tailwind CSS
Read the setup guide →
docker-compose.yml
services:
  familyplates:
    image: ghcr.io/elevate08/
      familyplates:latest
    ports:
      - "3000:80"
    volumes:
      - familyplates_data:/rails/storage
$ docker compose up -d ↵
