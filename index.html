Interactive Product Tour

Built around the way families actually eat.

Explore the core features designed to keep your kitchen organized, your grocery trips quick, and your entire household on the same page.

Weekly & Monthly Planner

See the whole week at a glance.

Plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner effortlessly across 7 days. Assign family cooks, add notes, and turn tonight's dinner into tomorrow's lunch.

  • 1-click recipe assignment across breakfast, lunch, and dinner
  • Cook assignments so kitchen responsibilities are crystal clear
  • Smart leftover scheduling to save money and reduce food waste
Recipe Vault & One-Click Import

Keep every family favorite close.

Import recipes from any food blog or cooking site with a single click. Browse by meal type, dietary tags, cook times, and ratings.

  • 1-click import extracts ingredients, times, and instructions
  • Filter by dinner, quick prep, vegetarian, kids' favorites, and custom tags
  • Household craving submissions to inspire weekly meal choices
1-Sheet Refrigerator View

A plan everyone can see on the fridge.

No screens required in the kitchen. Print a clean, high-contrast 1-page letter-landscape calendar designed specifically for hanging on the refrigerator.

  • High-contrast, ink-saving design for standard home printers
  • Fits the entire week on a single landscape sheet
  • Clear cook tags and meal names so anyone can check the plan
Smart Grocery Checklist

From weekly plan straight to supermarket aisle.

Ingredients from your planned meals are combined, quantified, and categorized by grocery aisle for frictionless in-store shopping on your phone.

  • Combines ingredient quantities across all weekly meals
  • Grouped by aisle: Produce, Dairy, Meat, Bakery, Spices, and Pantry
  • Mobile-optimized strike-off mode as you navigate the store
Smart Pantry Shield

Shop for what you need—not what you already own.

Keep track of staple pantry items like oils, flour, and spices. Items marked on hand are automatically deducted from your shopping list so you never buy duplicates.

  • Aisle-organized kitchen inventory of everyday staple ingredients
  • 1-tap toggle for on-hand stock status
  • Automatically excludes stocked staples from the weekly grocery list

Engineered for Everyday Family Life

Thoughtful details you'll appreciate every day.

No streaks, feeds, advertisements, or meal-planning theater. Just practical features that save time and reduce stress.

Real-Time Sync

Direct Google Calendar Integration

FamilyPlates writes scheduled meals, assigned cooks, preparation times, and ingredients straight to your shared family Google Calendar via Google Service Account in the background.

1-Click Import

Paste a Link, Get the Recipe

Drop in a link from NYT Cooking, AllRecipes, Serious Eats, or a favorite food blog. FamilyPlates pulls out the title, servings, cook time, and ingredients — and leaves the life story behind.

Personalization

Family Profiles & 12 Kitchen Themes

Give every household member their own avatar and select from 12 warm kitchen accent color palettes (Terracotta, Sage, Slate, Berry, Tuscan Sun, and more).

Kid-Safe

Admin Control & PIN Security

Kids can browse recipes and submit cravings freely on tablets, while meal schedule edits, roster changes, and system settings require a secure 4-digit PIN.

Installable PWA

Add It to Your Phone in One Tap

FamilyPlates is a Progressive Web App: open it once on iPhone or Android and tap Add to Home Screen. It launches full screen with its own icon — no app store, no install file — and the aisle-by-aisle grocery checklist is built for one-handed tapping in the store.

Data Privacy

Private SQLite Architecture

No tracking pixels, third-party analytics, or vendor lock-in. Your family recipes, meal histories, and schedules remain safely stored on your local hardware.
